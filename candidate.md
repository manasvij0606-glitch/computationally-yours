- Human behaviour & identity: *How much of our identity is our own, and how much is shaped by others?*

### Connections of things in them 



- Human behaviour & identity 

* **Reflection -  Identity and perception:** A reflection changes depending on the observer's position, raising questions about how we see ourselves versus how others see us.
* **Cloud -  Identity shaped by observation**: A cloud can look different to everyone who observes it, showing how identity can be shaped by perception.
* **Ink -  Identity as expression and representation**: Ink can communicate, represent or define someone's identity through words, images and labels.



- Relationships and memory

* **An old wall -  Relationships, memory and traces:** It retains marks and layers left by people long after they have moved on.
* **Fossil -  Evidence of a presence that no longer exists:** It preserves physical traces of life that have disappeared.
* **Ink -  Messages and stories that outlive their creators:** Written words can preserve someone's thoughts and presence long after they are gone.
* **Dust -  What accumulates after a presence disappears:** Dust gathers in spaces and on objects that have been left undisturbed, marking the passage of time.

- Nature

### Detailed Explorations

1\. Reflection



**Identity / Name:** Reflection\
**Time:** The present moment; it exists only while something is being reflected.\
**Place:** Reflective surfaces such as mirrors, glass and water.\
**Role:** Shows us something, but not necessarily exactly as it is.



A reflection changes depending on where you stand and what you are looking at. It makes me think about how we see ourselves versus how other people see us. Is the reflection just a copy, or does it become something different of its own? Also interested in distortion and how one thing can have multiple versions.



2\. An Old Wall



**Identity / Name:** An Old Wall\
**Time:** Past and present\
**Place:** Spaces that have been occupied, used or left behind like old buildings, abandoned spaces, lived-in places.\
**Role:** Holds marks and traces of what has happened around it.



A wall collects things over time — scratches, cracks, stains, layers of paint, things written or covered up. Even when the people who left these traces are gone, the marks remain. I am interested in what a wall might tell us about the people and events it has witnessed, and what happens when those traces slowly disappear.



3\. Static



**Identity / Name:** Static / Interference\
**Time:** During transmission, interruption or disruption.\
**Place:** Screens, radio signals, communication systems.\
**Role:** Noise, disruption, interference.



Static is usually seen as something meaningless, but what if there is something within the noise? It makes me think about interrupted communication and connections that are still there but cannot be understood. I am also interested in the point where noise starts to form patterns or something recognisable.



4\. Cloud



**Identity / Name:** Cloud\
**Time:** Constantly changing\
**Place:** The atmosphere and open sky.\
**Role:** Forms, moves, changes shape and disappears.



A cloud never really has one fixed shape. Two people can look at the same cloud and see completely different things. Its existence doesn't depend on anyone looking at it, but the way we interpret it does. I am interested in this difference, and in how something can keep changing without losing its existence.
